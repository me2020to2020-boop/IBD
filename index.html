Backup & transfer

Patients are autosaved on this device (this browser). To move the registry to another phone or hand it to a colleague, export a JSON file and import it there. Importing replaces the registry on the device you import into. Export includes IBD scores and endoscopy photos, so the file can be large if you attach many photos — keep an eye on the device storage and export a backup regularly.

Add to Home Screen — Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your phone.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top corner.
  3. Tap “Add to Home screen” (or “Install app”), then confirm.
  4. The registry icon appears on your home screen and opens like an app.

Add to Home Screen — iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this page in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow).
  3. Scroll down and tap “Add to Home Screen”, then tap Add.
  4. The registry icon appears on your home screen and opens like an app.

Note: the home-screen copy and the browser copy keep separate saved data on iPhone. Pick one copy for your patients and stick to it, and use Export JSON to move data between copies.
